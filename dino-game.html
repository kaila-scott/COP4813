How to play

Move with ← → or A D. On a phone, use the buttons below. Catch ferns and avoid rocks. After every three ferns, answer a nature question. Correct answers earn knowledge points. Every correct answer also uncovers a dinosaur fossil for your journal! The clock pauses while you answer or read your journal. Your discoveries are saved in this browser.

Why this prototype?

Fern & Fossil is an educational collection-and-trivia game. It teaches basic facts about dinosaurs, fossils, and prehistoric ecosystems while players collect ferns, dodge hazards, unlock fossil journal entries, and track their scores. The aim is easy to understand and suitable for future expansion.

Ideas for future updates

The fossil discovery journal is now playable and saves progress! Future ideas include age-adjusted question sets, new biomes, more dinosaurs, collectible seeds, power-ups, levels, and a forest restoration progress map. The game keeps its settings and object types organized in JavaScript so these can be added later.
