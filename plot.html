RC Circuit Charging Calculator

This calculator plots the voltage across a capacitor as it charges over time in an RC circuit. This is an exponential equation, not a linear, quadratic, or polynomial equation.

V(t) = Vs × (1 - e-t / RC)

Each point on the scatter plot shows the calculated capacitor voltage at a specific time. The connected curve helps show how the capacitor charges quickly at first and then slowly approaches the supply voltage.
