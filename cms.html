What I Learned

What surprised me most was that not every CMS depends on a traditional database. Grav and Statamic can use flat files for content, while Craft, ProcessWire, and Concrete use databases. The best choice depends on how much customization is needed, the development team's experience, and how easy the website must be for editors to maintain.

From a cybersecurity perspective, I would also consider how each CMS handles updates, permissions, plugins, and long-term maintenance. Even a flexible CMS can create problems if it is not kept updated or properly configured.
