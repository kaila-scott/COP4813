Rules, source, and modifications

AND and OR: This demo models a prerequisite as a list of required groups. All groups must be met (AND), while any course in a group may satisfy that group (OR). For example, the demo rule for CEN2002 is COP1000 AND (COP2360 OR COP2800).

Program choice: Choose one language course, COP2360 (C#) OR COP2800 (Java). Completing or planning one disables the other. Select up to three program electives.

My modifications: I chose a different degree program, designed a custom JSON structure, dynamically created the course cards and buttons, added AND/OR logic, course notes, drag-and-drop planning, a credit counter, and a copy-plan feature.

Important: This is a classroom demonstration, not an official advising or enrollment tool. The program course list comes from the current catalog, but the example prerequisite logic has not been verified against each course description. General education requirements are not modeled. Confirm actual eligibility and graduation requirements with DSC.
